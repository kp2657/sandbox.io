Well-Child Visit Checklist

Sub-I / student quick-reference tool — milestones, screening, vaccines, anticipatory guidance & visit log

For educational use — not a substitute for institutional protocol. Milestones and screening content follow the AAP Bright Futures periodicity schedule. As of 2026, the federal CDC immunization schedule and the AAP's published schedule diverge on several recommendations following a January 2026 change made outside the usual ACIP process; the vaccine lists below reflect the long-standing AAP/ACIP-style schedule and may not match your site's current EHR order set. Always confirm vaccines due against your clinic's current schedule and the parent's home records before counseling or ordering.

SSHADESS Interview (Adolescent Psychosocial) ▾
SSHADESS (Strengths, School, Home, Activities, Drugs, Emotions/Eating/dEpression, Sexuality, Safety) is Bright Futures' strengths-based adolescent psychosocial interview. Conduct confidentially, 1:1 without a parent in the room, starting around age 13 and at every visit through young adulthood. Only populated for adolescent/young-adult visit types. Prompt questions are shown for reference — write your own free-text summary of the conversation in each box.
